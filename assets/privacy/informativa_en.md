# Data processing notice

Version 2026-09-09

HealthyLog processes diet plans, body measurements and physical activity:
data concerning people's health. This notice describes which data are
processed, for what purposes, how long they are kept and who may be given
access to them.

## Which data are processed

- **Personal details**: first and last name, username, email address,
  date and place of birth, sex.
- **Diet plan data**: weekly schedule, meal content, recipes, notes,
  consumption ticks and swaps.
- **Body data**: height, weight, circumferences, target weight.
- **Physical activity**: recorded and planned workouts.
- **Technical data**: connected devices, time zone, language and unit
  system.

## For what purposes

The data are processed solely to run the service: viewing your plan,
recording consumption, workouts and measurements, viewing their
statistics, and — where you choose to — sharing what is needed with your
nutritionist or your cooking group.

The data are **not** used for profiling, aggregate analysis across users,
transfer to third parties or advertising.

## Who may be given access

Third-party access is always confined to the purpose that justifies it.
No role has general access.

- **The Cook and the group members** access only the data needed to
  prepare meals: slot content, consumption status, first and last name.
  Weight, measurements, workouts, statistics, history and the identity of
  any nutritionist are withheld from them.
- **The nutritionist** accesses only the data arising from their own
  professional work, for the periods covered by the plans they wrote.

Consent to the nutritionist's access is given by accepting the
professional link; consent to the Cook's access by joining the group.
Both are revocable at any time and without giving reasons.

## For how long

Data are kept as long as the account exists. Read notifications are
deleted automatically after thirty days; unread ones remain until you
take note of them.

## Deletion

You may request deletion of your account at any time. The request opens a
seven-day reconsideration period, during which the account is deactivated
and no third party accesses it, but the data are kept: signing in again
withdraws the request, with full restoration.

Once the period has passed, deletion is final and irreversible: plans,
occurrences, ticks, swap history, statistics, workouts, measurements,
templates, notifications and memberships are deleted.

## What the nutritionist keeps

Deletion does not extend to the documentation of the nutritionist's
professional work: the schedules of the plans they wrote and the
measurements they personally recorded. That documentation is stripped of
the person's identifying data: the nutritionist keeps the plan and their
own records, not the identity of who received them.

Retention answers the professional need to document one's own work, which
does not end when the relationship does.
